# Changelog

## v1.0.1

- Dictionary list: all Latin dictionaries installed in macOS plus Russian, chosen in the new
  **Languages** tab. The app no longer assumes an English/Russian pair — the alphabet of a layout
  is detected from the keys themselves.
- Developer information in the settings window and in the app bundle.

## v1.0.0

First release.

- Automatic layout correction based on the macOS spelling dictionaries.
- Typo correction that prefers the candidate closest on the keyboard.
- Forced conversion of the current or last word by shortcut.
- Selection actions: convert layout, invert case, transliterate.
- Undo of a replacement, with the reverted word remembered as an exception.
- Snippets, word exceptions and per-app exceptions.
- Settings window with a Check tab explaining the app's decision for any word.
